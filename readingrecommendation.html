Latest Recs

Snow White with the Red Hair

#001
赤髪の白雪姫 (Akagami no Shirayuki-hime)
Year: 2006
Media: Manga
Status: Ongoing
Story/Art: Sorata Akizuki
RomanceSlice of LifeFantasyDrama

Shirayuki's apple-red hair is famous, but not for reasons she'd like. The vain Prince Raji of Tanbarun wants her as his concubine, so she does the only logical thing which was cutting off her hair and bolts into the night.

Lost in the woods of the neighboring kingdom, she meets an injured boy named Zen and his two companions. After she heals his wounds, he returns the favor by helping her hide. This refreshing fantasy follows an optimistic heroine and a mysterious prince as they discover that sometimes the best escape route leads straight to a new beginning.

Baby Road Trippers

#002
ベイビー車中ハッカーズ (Baby Shachuhackers)
Year: 2024
Media: Manga
Status: Ongoing
Story/Art: Tabireko
Slice of LifeComedy

A young girl finds school suffocating and seeks freedom by sleeping in her car parked in her own backyard since she lacks a license.

Dreaming of driving wherever she pleases, her routine shifts when she befriends a bright classmate who can drive and opens new paths for both of them to escape together.

Dig It

#003
ディグイット (Dig It)
Year: 2025
Media: Manga
Status: Ongoing
Story/Art: Yoshidamaru
SportsDrama

As the son of a legendary volleyball ace, the boy shoulders expectations as his middle school team’s attacker but doubts his role.

When his father chooses to mentor a gifted teammate instead, he endured forging his own path, challenging both his rival and his father by redefining what it means to be an ace.

The Summit of the Gods

#004
神々の山嶺 (Kamigami no Itadaki)
Year: 2000
Media: Manga
Status: Completed
Story/Art: Baku Yumemakura, Jiro Taniguchi
DramaAdventurePsychologicalMystery

In 1993 a photo journalist discovers a camera possibly linked to George Mallory’s vanished 1924 Everest expedition.

Chasing the truth behind mountaineering’s greatest mystery, he follows the trail of a reclusive climber, uncovering a haunting tale of obsession, ambition and humanity’s relentless pull toward the summit.

The Creepy and Freaky

#005
こわいやさん (Kowaiyasan)
Year: 2025
Media: Manga
Status: Completed
Story/Art: Kamentotsu
HorrorSupernaturalMystery

Enter a weird store where fear is sold like trinkets. Browse at will and linger among chilling tales that prey on your imagination.

Each story is a curated terror of a slow burning dread as if whispers are inviting you to lose yourself in lingering horror and stay longer.
